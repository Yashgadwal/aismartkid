Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

Welcome to AI Smart Institute. We value the privacy of our students, parents, workshop attendees, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, protect, and use information when you interact with our website (aismartkid.vercel.app) and our physical training studio in Ujjain.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when booking a trial class, workshop, or counselling call, which may include:

  • Parent & Guardian Details: Full Name, Mobile / WhatsApp Number, Email Address.
  • Student Details: Full Name, Age Group, School / College, and learning goals.
  • Professional & Aspirant Details: Profession, Target Examination, City, and contact details.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, operating system, and website usage statistics via analytics.

2. Children's Privacy & Parental Consent (DPDP Rules, 2025)

In strict compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and Rules 2025, we are deeply committed to safeguarding the privacy of minors:

  • We collect personal details of individuals under 18 years of age only with verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian.
  • All children's data is strictly utilized for educational counseling, batch scheduling, workshop access, and academic progress updates.
  • We never sell, rent, or commercialize children's personal data, nor do we track children across third-party websites for targeted behavioral advertising.
  • Parents and guardians hold the complete right to review, update, or request the immediate deletion of their child's records at any time by contacting our administration.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data solely for educational, counseling, and administrative purposes, including:

  • Scheduling free trial sessions, workshops, and enrolling learners in batches.
  • Sending batch dates, schedules, payment receipts, or curriculum brochures via WhatsApp or Email.
  • Improving website usability, curriculum relevance, and educational material delivery.

4. Information Protection

We implement robust security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. We do not sell, trade, or transfer student or parent data to external commercial brokers. All lead submissions are stored securely and encrypted in transit.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We use standard performance cookies and privacy-respecting analytics tools to understand website traffic and improve user experience across mobile and desktop devices.
