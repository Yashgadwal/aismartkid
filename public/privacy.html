Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2, 2026

Welcome to AI Smart Kids. We value the privacy of our students, parents, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, protect, and use information when you interact with our website (aismartkid.vercel.app) and our institute in Ujjain.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when booking a trial class or requesting info, which may include:

  • Parent Details: Full Name, Mobile Number, Email Address.
  • Student Details: Age, School, Class Standard, and learning interests.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, operating system, and website usage statistics via analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data solely for educational and administrative purposes, including:

  • Scheduling free trial sessions and enrolling students in cohorts.
  • Sending batch updates, educational reminders, or course brochures via WhatsApp or Email.
  • Improving website layout and course content relevance.

3. Information Protection

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. We do not sell, trade, or transfer student or parent data to third parties. All lead details are stored securely inside our local database.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to analyze web traffic and help remember your preferences for future visits (such as login states for portals).
