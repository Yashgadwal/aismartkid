Our Philosophy

Crafting Cozy Moments & Delicious Memories in Ujjain

Located in the heart of Rishi Nagar, Cuddle Cafe is designed to be your cozy escape from the bustle. Inspired by premium minimalist aesthetics, we combine warm wood textures with vibrant green plants and comfortable spaces to create Ujjain's most Instagrammable destination.

Whether you're looking for a perfect romantic date spot, a lively table for birthday celebrations, a quiet corner to study, or a warm family hangout, Cuddle Cafe welcomes you with open arms. Every pizza is stretched by hand, every shake is whipped to perfection, and every cup of coffee is crafted to feel like a warm hug.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your cafe operating hours?

We are open daily from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM, including weekends. Last kitchen orders are taken at 10:45 PM.

Do you accept online table reservations?

Yes, you can reserve a table directly using our online booking form below or by clicking the WhatsApp floating assistant. Reservations are free of charge.

How can I book the banquet hall?

You can fill out the Banquet inquiry form below or call us directly. We accommodate up to 60 guests and provide customized menu templates for birthdays, anniversaries, and small gatherings.

Is parking available at the Rishi Nagar location?

Yes, we have dedicated roadside four-wheeler and two-wheeler parking available in front of the cafe in Rishi Nagar, Ujjain.
